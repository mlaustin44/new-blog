I'm Matthew Austin. I build the systems that deploy and operate robots at scale: getting software onto machines in the field, keeping it current, and knowing what every one of them is doing.

Off the clock I build things, fix cars, read more than is sensible and cook passably. I like any problem that is new to me, and anything I can take apart to see how it works.

## This site

Everything here falls into one of three kinds:

- **Work** is professional: case studies of things I built and shipped.
- **Writing** is engineering notes and longer essays.
- **Projects** is what I build for its own sake.

Each entry has a number. They are assigned in order and never reused, so a reference to N-012 will always mean the same thing.
