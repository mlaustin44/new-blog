A factory service manual assumes you are standing at the machine with your hands full. Every step names the tool, the fastener and the torque. Most runbooks assume you are sitting comfortably and already know the system.

## Write for the person holding the wrench

The reader of a runbook is tired, interrupted and mid-incident. Give them the next action first, the reason second, and the background last.
